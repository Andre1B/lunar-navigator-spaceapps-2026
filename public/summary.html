Compare an original arrival, a delayed arrival and an alternative point. The prototype includes Connecting ridge, Shackleton rim, Nobile rim 1 and Malapert massif, with 0.5, 1, 2, 5 and 10 m observer heights, from 1 March to 28 April 2027. These points in published study areas are not certified landing sites.

Inspect mean visible solar-disc fraction, longest darkness, time with some Earth visible and the longest Earth-visibility gap. Mean illumination uses hourly samples; events are searched at five-minute steps and boundaries refined. Shorter events may be missed, and boundary-touching intervals are labeled as lower bounds.

Limitations, rights and AI assistance

Earth visibility is not an operational radio link, DSN schedule or relay model. Sunlight is not electrical power or battery survival. The globe's day/night shading is distinct from numerical terrain visibility. This educational prototype does not certify landing safety or claim NASA endorsement.

Original code is Apache-2.0. Third-party data and software retain their own terms; source links and notices are public. Claude and Codex assisted research, implementation, explanatory text and verification. Numerical outputs come from code processing cited data, not LLM-invented values. See AI-use metadata. Interpretation of the earlier AI-assisted ideation history remains unresolved; no eligibility certification is claimed.

Review and reproduce

The three-minute review connects the question, your current scenario results and executed evidence. Compare shows a user-defined darkness limit, preserving lower-bound uncertainty. Its downloadable JSON records UTC windows, summaries, coordinates, source provenance, derived-data checksums, build identity and limitations. Each metric links to its explanation. Ideas from 24 previous global winners informed these independently implemented improvements; no competitor code was copied.

New in this release candidate

  • Next transitions: the next sunrise, sunset, earthrise and earthset above the terrain horizon after the displayed instant, read from refined events (5-minute search, at most 1-minute refinement).
  • Arrival skies: original and delayed arrival skies side by side at the same point and height.
  • Arrival-date series: every arrival date that fits the computed period for the selected point, with visible lower bounds. It shows variation and does not rank dates or points.
  • Worked example with real data: at Malapert massif, raising the observer from 1 m to 5 m shortens the longest period without direct Sun from 289 h to 144 h (1 March to 28 April 2027, dataset values).
  • Text description: a polite screen-reader description of the Sun and Earth relative to the terrain horizon, updated when time stops.

Motion that explains the experiment

A skippable spacecraft-to-logo opening, finite page motion and a controllable four-step 2D walkthrough introduce the tools. Reduced motion skips the automatic opening. Inspect five minutes before/after the calculated events. Live values stay visible during playback and carry their own frame time.

Display-refresh rendering has ceilings of 60 FPS mobile and 120 desktop, not a guarantee of device performance. Optional light paths are diagrams, not visible vacuum beams or ray tracing. Detailed shadows and optical glare change appearance only.

Understand it with one change.

On Home, select +3, +7 or +14 days to compare real visibility times immediately. Open that exact experiment to change the place, date or height. Six calculated points include two neighbors selected by fixed offsets of about 1 km; a nearby-alternative button preserves your other assumptions.

The opening ends on Home; opening the simulator starts playback. Reduced-motion, tours and exact inspections start paused. Look at Sun and Look at Earth continuously follow the current direction; drag to look freely. All scientific inputs, physical scales and horizon calculations stay unchanged.

Optional ambient music

Enable Music in the header for an original browser-synthesized ambient score. Volume and pause remain under your control; music starts off, continues across screens, and uses no external recordings or samples. It is an artistic soundscape, not sonification of scientific data.

Choose a UTC arrival with the custom calendar or type YYYY-MM-DD. Days are limited to the computed period; windows outside it are not calculated. Optional original music adds bass pulses and arpeggios, with volume and pause controls.
